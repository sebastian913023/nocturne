04 · VOICE

Calm, not hyped

We describe autonomy plainly — "ran a full cycle," "flagged for approval" — never breathless marketing verbs.

Nocturnal metaphor, used sparingly

Night/dawn language (morning report, night cycle) anchors the brand — don't stack more than one per sentence.

Founder-first

Copy always centers the human's decision ("needs you," "your call") — the AI proposes, the founder decides.
