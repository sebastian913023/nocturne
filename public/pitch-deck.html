The problem

Most business ideas die from operations, not lack of demand.

Naming, a domain, a landing page, payments, a first ad, a support inbox, a bookkeeping habit — none of it is hard on its own. Together it's enough friction that most ideas never leave a notes app.

Time
A single founder juggling ten disciplines has no nights or weekends left for the idea itself.
Cost
Hiring a co-founder, agency, or freelancer for each piece is expensive before there's a dollar of revenue.
Trust
Existing "AI builder" tools generate a one-off artifact — a site, a doc — then stop. Nobody runs the business afterward.
02 / 12
The solution

An AI co-founder — not another generator.

You describe an idea in one sentence. Nocturne names it, builds the site and checkout, wires up a small team of specialized agents, and keeps running the business every night — surfacing a morning report and pausing on anything that needs your OK.

  • No team, no code, no dashboards to configure — you bring the idea, Nocturne brings the 3am shift.
  • Human-in-the-loop by default: spend, publishing, and outreach can all require founder approval.
  • Aligned incentives: Nocturne only takes a cut of revenue it helps generate.
